Before you order

Keep VigorBuy product links traceable

For each find, save the original product listing URL, marketplace name, seller name, item ID, selected colorway and date checked. If you copy a Weidian product link, a Taobao seller listing or a 1688 item URL, confirm that your agent supports it and that the color, size and pack quantity match your notes.

Compare sizes in centimeters

Use this VigorBuy size guide alongside the seller’s size chart. For sneakers, compare foot length against the model-specific chart and check an insole measurement when available. For tees and hoodies, compare flat chest width, shoulder width and body length with a garment you own. For pants, check waistband width, rise and inseam.

Read VigorBuy QC photos by variant

Compare warehouse QC photos with the colorway and size on your order. Check the shoe outsole, heel shape and size label, or look at garment seam stitching, zipper alignment and fabric close-ups. For headwear, look for a photo of the rear strap. For jewelry, ask for a clasp close-up and a length measurement. Confirm the return window before requesting changes.

Check the shipping estimate for your address

For a VigorBuy shipping estimate, enter your destination country and postcode, then compare quotes using the packed parcel weight and box dimensions. Check how the carrier calculates chargeable weight and whether the quote includes insurance or delivery surcharges. Keep the item subtotal, domestic delivery fee and international shipping quote as separate amounts.

Frequently asked questions

What is a VigorBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shortlist of product links and notes for comparing items, sizes and shipping estimates. This independent guide links to HipoBuyFind categories for browsing.

Can I order VigorBuy finds from this page?

This page directs you to HipoBuyFind. For agent-assisted ordering, verify the source listing and confirm that your chosen agent can process the exact item and variant.

Can QC photos prove an item is authentic?

No. QC photos can show visible condition, measurements and variant details. For authenticity, verify the seller through the brand or use an independent authentication service.

What if the product link is broken or the size is sold out?

Search the catalog using the item name or model code. Compare the new listing’s seller, size chart and variant photos before adding it to your shortlist.

When can I compare international shipping quotes?

Use the packed parcel weight, dimensions and destination postcode for a closer comparison. Check the available services and what each quote includes before choosing a shipping line.
